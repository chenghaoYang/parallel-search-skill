## conflicts
- exactly-once wording: AWS claims Standard "exactly-once" (C8) while DBOS splits it — transactions "exactly once" but steps "at least once" (C15), and Temporal only recommends idempotency (C27). Same term, different scope (engine-internal vs end-effects).
- "open source" marketing vs license reality: Inngest/Restate called open source in marketing/Show HN while SSPL/BSL are not OSI-approved (C11/C13 vs C29).
