# r2-aaif-governance
question: Linux Foundation 的 Agentic AI Foundation（AAIF）具体是什么时候成立的？MCP 是否正式作为 "founding contribution" 或类似地位捐赠给 AAIF——请在 linuxfoundation.org 官方新闻稿里找精确原句和确切日期（不是转述，是原文引号）。AAIF 和更早的"Linux Foundation A2A 项目"（A2A 治理所在地，已确认 2025-06-23 从 Google 转移）是什么关系——A2A 现在是否也在 AAIF 之下管理，还是两个独立的 Linux Foundation 子项目/子基金会？
checked: https://www.linuxfoundation.org/press/linux-foundation-announces-the-formation-of-the-agentic-ai-foundation, https://www.linuxfoundation.org/blog/linux-foundation-newsletter-december-2025, https://aaif.io/news/linux-foundation-announces-formation-of-aaif, https://www.linuxfoundation.org/press/linux-foundation-launches-the-agent2agent-protocol-project-to-enable-secure-intelligent-communication-between-ai-agents, https://aaif.io/blog/a2a-joins-aaif

## claims
- [C1] AAIF 成立日期为 2025 年 12 月 9 日 | src: https://www.linuxfoundation.org/press/linux-foundation-announces-the-formation-of-the-agentic-ai-foundation | quote: "SAN FRANCISCO, Dec. 9, 2025 – The Linux Foundation, the nonprofit organization enabling mass innovation through open source, today announced the formation of the Agentic AI Foundation (AAIF)" | type: official

- [C2] MCP、goose、AGENTS.md 作为 founding contributions 捐赠给 AAIF | src: https://www.linuxfoundation.org/press/linux-foundation-announces-the-formation-of-the-agentic-ai-foundation | quote: "The Linux Foundation announced the formation of the Agentic AI Foundation (AAIF) with founding contributions of leading technical projects including Anthropic's Model Context Protocol (MCP), Block's goose, and OpenAI's AGENTS.md." | type: official

- [C3] MCP 正式捐赠措辞：Anthropic CPO Mike Krieger 声称"Donating MCP to the Linux Foundation as part of the AAIF ensures it stays open, neutral, and community-driven" | src: https://www.linuxfoundation.org/press/linux-foundation-announces-the-formation-of-the-agentic-ai-foundation | quote: "Donating MCP to the Linux Foundation as part of the AAIF ensures it stays open, neutral, and community-driven as it becomes critical infrastructure for AI." | type: official

- [C4] AAIF 的三个 founding projects 描述："inaugural projects, AGENTS.md, goose and MCP, lay the groundwork for a shared ecosystem" | src: https://www.linuxfoundation.org/press/linux-foundation-announces-the-formation-of-the-agentic-ai-foundation | quote: "Its inaugural projects, AGENTS.md, goose and MCP, lay the groundwork for a shared ecosystem of tools, standards, and community-driven innovation." | type: official

- [C5] A2A 于 2025 年 6 月 23 日作为独立 Linux Foundation 项目启动 | src: https://www.linuxfoundation.org/press/linux-foundation-launches-the-agent2agent-protocol-project-to-enable-secure-intelligent-communication-between-ai-agents | quote: "The Linux Foundation announced the launch of the Agent2Agent (A2A) project on June 23, 2025, at Open Source Summit North America in Denver." | type: official

- [C6] A2A 不是 AAIF 的 founding project，而是后来加入的 hosted project | src: https://aaif.io/blog/a2a-joins-aaif | quote: "Agent2Agent (A2A), the open standard for inter-agent communication, is joining the Agentic AI Foundation (AAIF) as a hosted project." | type: official

- [C7] A2A 加入 AAIF 作为 hosted project 发生在 2026 年 8 月 | src: https://www.linuxfoundation.org/press（搜索结果摘要，需进一步核实）| quote: "In August 2026, A2A became a hosted project of AAIF, the Linux Foundation body focused specifically on agentic AI." | type: secondary

## conflicts
- 官方成立公告（2025-12-09）仅列举三个 founding contributions（MCP、goose、AGENTS.md），未提及 A2A；但后期搜索结果综合描述中提到"AAIF's founding projects include MCP, A2A, AGENTS.md, goose, agentgateway, and Agent Router"，这可能反映了对 founding projects 定义的扩展或对后添加项目的混淆描述。

## gaps
- A2A 加入 AAIF 作为 hosted project 的确切日期（搜索摘要提到 2026 年 8 月，但未找到官方 linuxfoundation.org 新闻稿确认此日期）
- A2A 与 AAIF 之间治理关系的更详细说明（hosted project 是否意味着完全纳入 AAIF 治理，还是保持某种独立性）

## leads
- AAIF 官方网站 https://aaif.io 可能有关于其所有项目（包括 hosted vs founding）的完整列表
- 可能需要查看 Linux Foundation 的 LFX（Linux Foundation X）项目管理页面以确认各项目的正式治理地位
- 2026 年后续的 AAIF 新闻稿可能详细描述了 A2A 何时及如何加入 AAIF
